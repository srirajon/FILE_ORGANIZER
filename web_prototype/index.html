🖥️ Access Files on Your PC

Method 1: Direct USB Cable (Fastest)

1. Plug your phone into your PC with a USB cable.

2. Open Windows File Explorer → This PC\[Your Phone]\Internal Storage\Documents\FileOrganizer\

3. You will see every folder as a regular Windows folder with all files and .md notes ready to open!

Method 2: Google Drive Web

1. In the app, tap Sync with Google Drive.

2. On your PC, open drive.google.com.

3. Access the FileOrganizer folder to view, download, or edit files.

Method 3: Standalone Backup Package

1. Tap Export Backup Package.

2. Send the .fo_backup file to your PC via WhatsApp Web or email.
